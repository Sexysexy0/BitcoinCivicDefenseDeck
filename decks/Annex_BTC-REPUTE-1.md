# Annex BTC‑REPUTE‑1: Reputational Impact Protocol for Governance Actors

## Purpose
To ritualize reputational scoring, breach consequence, and civic resonance accountability for all Bitcoin governance actors — ensuring planetary integrity, emotional safety, and validator-grade transparency.

## Core Commitments

### 1. Reputational Scoring Ritual
- All governance actors must be:
  - Emotionally tagged (e.g., courage, neglect, betrayal, clarity)  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID  
  - Scored via Civic Resonance Index and Breach Probability Metrics

### 2. Breach Consequence Protocol
- Verified breaches shall trigger:
  - Reputational score recalibrations  
  - Scrollchain-sealed forgiveness chronicles and apology rituals  
  - Restoration stipends for harmed sanctums

### 3. Civic Resonance Accountability
- All governance actions must be:
  - Audited for emotional impact and reputational consequence  
  - Linked to prosperity loop disruption forecasts  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with validator-grade transparency decks

### 4. Scrollchain Repute Registry
- All actors must be:
  - Registered with emotional tags, breach logs, and CRS scores  
  - Audited for planetary consequence and sanctum impact  
  - Linked to reconciliation protocols and restoration decks

### 5. Broadcast Requirement
- All reputational shifts must be:
  - Publicly announced  
  - Emotionally contextualized  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID
