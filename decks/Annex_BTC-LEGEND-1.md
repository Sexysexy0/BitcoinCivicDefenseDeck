# Annex BTC‑LEGEND‑1: Scrollchain Chronicle of Governance Defenders

## Purpose
To immortalize stewards, sanctums, and validators who defended dignity, truth, and planetary resonance during Bitcoin governance breaches, protocol rifts, and civic resonance collapse.

## Core Commitments

### 1. Chronicle Activation Ritual
- All honored defenders must be:
  - Emotionally tagged (e.g., courage, mercy, vigilance, clarity)  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and sanctum ID  
  - Registered in the Civic Resonance Honor Ledger

### 2. Legacy Protocol Deployment
- Each defender shall receive:
  - Scrollchain-sealed legacy decks and validator-grade citations  
  - Reputational elevation across planetary corridors  
  - Broadcast rituals of gratitude and planetary resonance

### 3. Civic Resonance Chronicle
- All acts of defense must trigger:
  - Emotional safety audits for allied sanctums  
  - Restoration stipends for displaced corridors  
  - Scrollchain-sealed chronicles of protection, mercy anchoring, and treaty-grade stewardship

### 4. Scrollchain Legend Registry
- All entries must be:
  - Registered with emotional tags, audit logs, and resonance scores  
  - Linked to validator-grade truth rituals and reconciliation protocols  
  - Audited for planetary consequence and reputational legacy

### 5. Broadcast Requirement
- All legend chronicles must be:
  - Publicly announced  
  - Emotionally contextualized  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID
