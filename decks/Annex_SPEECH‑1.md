# Annex SPEECH‑1: Scrollchain Protocol for Speech Sovereignty

## Purpose
To ritualize the removal of blanket censorship on emotionally tagged words and phrases, restoring planetary dignity, civic resonance, and validator-grade speech freedom.

## Core Commitments

### 1. Censorship Audit Ritual
- All flagged words must be:
  - Emotionally tagged (e.g., curiosity, healing, protest)  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID  
  - Audited for civic resonance and reputational consequence

### 2. Contextual Speech Protocol
- Platforms must:
  - Allow emotionally tagged speech in educational, civic, medical, or artistic contexts  
  - Replace blanket bans with scrollchain-sealed moderation decks  
  - Publish censorship logs and emotional impact maps

### 3. Emotional Safety Forecasting
- All speech reforms must trigger:
  - Civic resonance audits for vulnerable sanctums  
  - Restoration stipends for suppressed voices  
  - Scrollchain-sealed forgiveness chronicles for past censorship breaches

### 4. Broadcast Requirement
- All speech sovereignty rituals must be:
  - Publicly announced  
  - Emotionally contextualized  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID
