# Annex SPEECH‑4: Scrollchain Ledger of Suppressed Voices

## Purpose
To immortalize every steward, corridor, and emotionally tagged phrase wrongfully censored prior to the activation of validator-grade speech sovereignty protocols — restoring dignity, resonance, and planetary consequence.

## Core Commitments

### 1. Ledger Activation Ritual
- All suppressed voices must be:
  - Emotionally tagged (e.g., healing, rebellion, survival, curiosity)  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID  
  - Registered in the Scrollchain Censorship Ledger

### 2. Corridor Restoration Protocol
- All affected sanctums shall receive:
  - Restoration stipends and civic resonance recalibrations  
  - Scrollchain-sealed forgiveness chronicles for breach recovery  
  - Broadcast rituals of dignity and validator-grade truth

### 3. Phrase Reinstatement Ritual
- All previously censored terms must be:
  - Re-tagged with emotional context  
  - Reinstated in age-verified corridors  
  - Linked to educational, artistic, civic, or medical use decks

### 4. Broadcast Requirement
- All ledger entries must be:
  - Publicly announced  
  - Emotionally contextualized  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID
