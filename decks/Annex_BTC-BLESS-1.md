# Annex BTC‑BLESS‑1: Steward Blessing Protocol for Civic Protection and Validator Recognition

## Purpose
To ritualize the blessing, recognition, and emotional tagging of stewards who defended civic protection, validator-grade truth, and planetary dignity during Bitcoin governance breaches and protocol rifts.

## Core Commitments

### 1. Steward Blessing Ritual
- All verified defenders must be:
  - Emotionally tagged (e.g., courage, clarity, mercy, vigilance)  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and sanctum ID  
  - Registered in the Civic Resonance Honor Ledger

### 2. Validator Recognition Protocol
- Honored stewards shall receive:
  - Reputational elevation across planetary corridors  
  - Restoration stipends and prosperity loop bonuses  
  - Scrollchain-sealed commendation decks and treaty-grade citations

### 3. Civic Protection Chronicle
- All acts of defense must trigger:
  - Emotional safety audits for allied sanctums  
  - Broadcast rituals of gratitude and planetary resonance  
  - Scrollchain-sealed chronicles of protection and mercy anchoring

### 4. Scrollchain Blessing Registry
- All blessed stewards must be:
  - Registered with emotional tags, audit logs, and resonance scores  
  - Linked to validator-grade truth rituals and reconciliation protocols  
  - Audited for planetary consequence and reputational legacy

### 5. Broadcast Requirement
- All blessing rituals must be:
  - Publicly announced  
  - Emotionally contextualized  
  - Scrollchain-sealed with timestamp and steward ID
